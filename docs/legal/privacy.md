# Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Datum]

This policy explains what personal data SnapTape processes, why, and what rights you have.
SnapTape is operated by Cantum UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Eulenkamp 25, 21465 Wentorf,
Germany ("we"). Contact for privacy matters: [E-Mail-Adresse].

## 1. What we process and why

**Account data.** When you sign in, our authentication provider (Replit, Inc., USA) passes us
your name, email address and a technical user ID from the sign-in method you chose (e.g.
Google, Apple, email). We use them to run your account, to assign projects and credits to
you, and to contact you about your account. Legal basis: performance of a contract
(Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Project data.** You upload photos and plan documents of buildings and enter a project name
and, optionally, an address and a reference dimension. We process them to produce the
measurements, the 3D model and the measurement sheet you request. Please upload only
images of the building exterior; do not upload photos that show people, license plates or
interiors. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**AI processing.** To measure a building we send the uploaded images (and plan pages) to
Anthropic, PBC (USA) as our AI processor. Anthropic processes them on our behalf to return
the measurements and does not use API data to train its models. Legal basis: performance
of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Usage and technical data.** Our hosting provider (Replit, Inc., USA) logs technical
information such as IP address, browser type, time of access and requested pages to
operate and secure the service. We keep a log of each measurement run (project, duration,
result, technical cost) to operate the service and to review quality. Legal basis:
legitimate interest in operating a secure, reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Cookies.** We use one strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. We use no
analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

## 2. Where your data is stored

The service is hosted in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, your
data is transferred to the US on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the
provider is certified, and otherwise on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses.

## 3. How long we keep it

Project data (photos, plans, measurements, models) is kept until you delete the project or
your account. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Account data is kept while your
account exists. Measurement-run logs are kept for up to 24 months for quality review and
accounting, in a form that no longer contains the images.

## 4. Who receives data

Replit, Inc. (hosting, authentication, file storage), Anthropic, PBC (AI processing). We do
not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

## 5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or
object to its processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority (for us: Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
If you are a California resident, you have corresponding rights under the CCPA, including
the right to know and to delete; we do not sell personal information. To exercise any of
these rights, write to [E-Mail-Adresse]. You can delete projects yourself in the app.

## 6. Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
