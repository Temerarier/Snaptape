GOAL
Fix /viewer-next's mobile/tablet layout, label collisions, color system, panel clutter,
and 3D rendering quality — including that the model is currently a hollow, open-bottomed
shell visible from below. This is a polish and correctness pass, not new features. The
desktop (>=1280px) side-by-side structure and all measurement logic stay untouched — only
tokens, the removals named below, and 3D presentation there. Card spacing/padding is
already correct - do not change it.

CONTEXT
- docs/viewer-spec.md sections 9-11 (model overlays, viewport controls, screen sizes).
- docs/design/viewer-v4-tokens.md for the color/type scale.
- /design/viewer-v4-desktop, the existing design export, for the background tone and the
  card treatment.
- Attached: (1) the existing round button cluster - use it as the component style for the
  phone controls; (2) a low/rear camera angle showing the model is hollow from underneath;
  (3) the Claude design reference viewer, showing dimension labels placed beside the house
  rather than over it, and a translucent selection fill rather than an outline.
- Known issues below come from a static code audit plus visual review of live screenshots -
  trust the screenshots over the audit's guesses about what "looks fine."

BUILD

1. REPLACE THE DRAG-SHEET WITH TWO FIXED STATES (phone + tablet portrait)
   Remove the 72/42/12dvh drag-detent system entirely - no drag handle, no swipe-to-resize.
   Older users on a job site will trigger drag gestures by accident while scrolling the
   panel, and the 12dvh state currently leaves them with almost no visible model and no
   obvious way back.
   - Default state: fixed ~50/50 split, model on top, panel below. Panel scrolls
     independently; model never scrolls.
   - One explicit toggle button, always visible, on the divider between model and panel,
     labelled ("Show full model" / "Show measurements"). It switches between the split view
     and a full-height view of just the model or just the panel. Two states only, both
     reached by a single tap, never a drag.
   - Model row must never compute to less than 260px tall in the split state, at any
     supported phone height down to 667px (iPhone SE class). If the math would go lower,
     widen the model row's share of the screen instead of shrinking further.

2. PHONE CONTROLS: ROUND CLUSTER, TOP RIGHT
   Phone (viewport width < 768px) gets its own control treatment instead of reusing
   tablet-portrait's text header:
   - A vertical cluster of round, icon-only 44x44px buttons anchored at the TOP RIGHT of
     the model area: measure, conditions, reset - in that order, as the attached
     button-cluster screenshot shows. Reuse that existing component/style if it is already
     in the codebase rather than inventing a new one.
   - This means the measure control keeps the same ANCHOR (top right of the model area) at
     every breakpoint, but changes FORM: a labelled pill on desktop and tablet, the top
     round button of the cluster on phone. Do not move it to a different corner or to the
     bottom on any breakpoint.
   - A bottom calc bar (full width, docked to the bottom edge of the screen) instead of the
     floating top-centre calc bubble, so it never overlaps the model or dimension labels.
     Desktop and tablet keep the calc bubble.
   - "+ Add photo" stays in the header, compacted, and the header must not wrap to a second
     line at 390px.
   - Gesture hint text must never overlap the model or other controls; hide it after first
     successful interaction or after a few seconds, whichever the codebase already
     supports elsewhere.

3. FIX LABEL COLLISIONS AND PLACEMENT (all breakpoints, worst on phone)
   Currently permanent dimension labels (width/ridge/eave) and the selection/attribute
   tooltip can render on top of the house and on top of each other - visible in the current
   screenshots at both desktop and phone widths (a VENT tooltip sitting on top of a ridge
   dimension label, dimension labels crossing over the model itself).
   - Reposition permanent dimension labels so they sit BESIDE the house, never over it:
     the width dimension on its own dimension line below the building, and ridge and eave
     on a single shared vertical height axis to the right of the building, each with a
     dashed extension line running back to the edge it measures. The Claude design
     reference (attachment 3) shows this arrangement.
   - Extend the existing selection-box collision/placement logic (right/left/below/above
     candidate evaluation) to also treat OTHER visible dimension labels as obstacles, not
     just `data-control` elements.
   - The selection tooltip sits beside its element with a thin connector line to an anchor
     dot on the element - never covering the element it describes.
   - Permanent dimension labels need the same edge-clamping the selection box already has
     (keep them fully inside the exposed viewport, never centered off-edge).
   - On phone, scale dimension/tooltip label font down one step (17px -> 14px) so they take
     less of the now-small model area; keep the 12px hard minimum from the tokens file.
   - If two labels would still collide after clamping, offset the lower-priority one (the
     transient tooltip beats the permanent dimension label) rather than letting them overlap.

4. FIX THE "COPIED" TOAST POSITION
   It currently renders centered over the model, covering the house on every screen size.
   Move it to a small toast docked at the bottom edge of the model area (or bottom of
   screen on phone), auto-dismissing, never covering the house or its labels.

5. COLOR SYSTEM: ADOPT THE BLUE-TINTED BACKGROUND, REMOVE LITERAL COLORS
   - Change the app shell background and the 3D scene background from the current near-
     white (#fafbfc / #f7fafc) to a blue-tinted background matching the tone of the
     /design/viewer-v4-desktop export and the Claude design reference. Add that export's
     subtle background grid if present in its markup - it gives the 3D scene depth cues it
     currently lacks against flat white.
   - Audit every literal hex color in the viewer (measure red #991b1b, selection box
     #101820/#cbd5e1, permanent line #334155, hemisphere light #aab6c4, calc border/chip
     colors, and any Tailwind slate/red/emerald utility classes used for viewer UI) and
     replace each with a value from docs/design/viewer-v4-tokens.md, or add it to that file
     as a new named token if genuinely needed and note that addition in your report.
   - Keep the existing colored left-rail treatment on measurement cards (roof/edges/gutters
     etc.) and the existing card spacing/padding exactly as they are - both already match
     what we want, do not touch them.

6. PANEL CLEANUP AND FILTER PILLS
   - DELETE THE "5 references · 5 warnings" ROW: the summary row and its expandable detail
     at the top of the panel, above the filters and the cards, is removed entirely - not
     moved, not collapsed, deleted. The quality/warnings block that already sits at the
     BOTTOM of the panel covers this information and stays exactly as it is; the row at the
     top was duplicating it and pushing the measurement numbers down. After this change the
     panel reads: "Measurements" heading, filter pills, cards, existing bottom quality block.
   - Filter pills (All/Roofing/Siding/Painting) KEEP their 44px height - touch targets stay
     44px everywhere, no exceptions. Increase the horizontal GAP between the pills instead
     so the row reads as separate filters rather than one solid block.
   - Reduce the model stage's reserved control space (currently 80-96px) now that controls
     are docked (see #2/#3) instead of floating - free that space back to the model.
   - Do not change card-to-card gaps or card internal padding - leave those exactly as they
     are today.

7. CLEAN UP THE 3D RENDERING: CLOSE THE VOLUME, KEEP THE CAMERA FREE, FILL THE SELECTION
   Current model reads as flat/cluttered - hatched window textures, harsh flat shading,
   visibly busy roof icon clutter - and, more seriously, the building is a hollow, open
   shell: any low or rear camera angle looks straight through the missing bottom into the
   dark interior faces of the walls and roof underside (see attachment 2). Fix all of the
   below. The camera must stay fully free to orbit in every direction, including underneath
   the model - do not add any polar-angle or ground-clamp restriction to `OrbitControls`.
   The fix for the see-through problem is a closed volume, not a locked camera.
   - CLOSE THE BOTTOM: add a floor/foundation cap face at z=0 (or the model's base
     elevation) spanning the building's footprint, so the volume reads as solid from any
     camera angle including from below. This can be a simple flat opaque polygon per
     footprint outline (including any attachments/garage footprint) - it does not need
     texture or material fidelity, it only needs to stop the see-through.
   - Check for other open seams in the same pass: gaps at wall-to-wall corners, wall-to-roof
     joins, and the garage/attachment-to-main-volume seam, wherever a rear or low angle
     could currently see through into the interior. Close any found.
   - SELECTION HIGHLIGHT: change the selected-element highlight in the 3D scene from an
     outline-only treatment to a translucent, light-blue filled overlay on the selected
     face/element (as the Claude design reference shows) - a semi-transparent blue material
     applied over the selected geometry, not just an edge line. Keep the edge accent if it
     reads well combined with the fill; the fill is the required addition.
   - Soften materials: replace the hatched window pattern with a simple semi-transparent
     blue-tinted glass material (flat fill, subtle opacity, no hatch texture) unless the
     hatching is meaningfully load-bearing data (i.e. representing a real condition/damage
     overlay - keep it only there, remove it from plain window fill).
   - Soften lighting: reduce the current hemisphere (1.9) and directional (2.2) intensities
     to something less harsh, and/or add a second, weaker fill light so shaded faces aren't
     flat black - target the softer, more even shading of the reference illustration,
     without needing a full PBR/shadow-map pipeline.
   - Add a ground contact shadow: a simple soft radial/elliptical shadow blob (a
     transparent-edged plane with a gradient or blurred texture) positioned under the
     house's footprint, darkest directly under the building and fading out - not a full
     real-time shadow-mapped light, just a baked/decal-style contact shadow for visual
     grounding. Scale it to the model's footprint (bounds.widthMm x bounds.depthMm).
   - Do not change wall/roof/opening geometry, camera framing/controls, or any
     measurement-driven logic beyond what's needed to close the volume - materials,
     lighting, the floor cap, the selection fill, and the added shadow plane only.
